Basics of Stars and the Earth

What do astronomers usually do?

They Identify a problem/phenomena

Introduce a model/hypothesis and make predictions for further phenomena that would be seen is the model is correct.

Conduct experiments or observations to test the predictions.

Publish theories/papers so that they can be checked/criticized.This helps in providing info about when and where the model needs to be revised.

Constellations

Area on THE CELESTIAL SPHERE in which a group of a visible from a percieved pattern/outline, typically representing Inanimate object, Mythological Subject, Animal.

Acc. to modern star charts, there are 88 officially recognized constellations.

The CLOSENESS of the constellations are majorly because of the availability of them in the SAME DIRECTION as viewed from Earth

Annual Motion

The star appears to "slowly shift" throughout the year. This is due to the ELLIPTICAL ORBIT of the Earth around the Sun.

A particular star rises 4 mins earlier every night, 2 hours earlier every month.

Mapping the sky

Celestial Equator -> divides sky into northern hemisphere and southern hemisphere

Celestial Poles -> Points where the earth's axis of rotation would intersect the celestial sphere

Now, why is POLARIS called the POLE STAR/NORTH STAR?

Polaris is 1 degree away from north celestial pole, which is why it is called the NORTH STAR.

Points in the sky directly overhead an observer an observer anywhere on Earth is called the observer's ZENITH.
